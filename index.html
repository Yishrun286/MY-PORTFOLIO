THE DESIGNER
BEHIND JESH

I'm Yishrun Legesse — a graphic designer and Software Engineering student based in Ethiopia. I founded Jesh Projects to bring high-end visual storytelling to YouTube creators and Ethiopian businesses who want their brand to command attention.

My work sits at the intersection of data-driven design and cinematic aesthetics. Every thumbnail I build is engineered to convert — not just look good. I understand creator psychology, viewer behavior, and what makes someone stop scrolling and click.

What sets me apart: I combine design instincts with technical coding skills. I don't just make things look good — I make them perform.
